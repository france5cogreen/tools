What is Unix time?

Unix time counts the seconds elapsed since 1970-01-01 00:00:00 UTC (the epoch), ignoring leap seconds. It is timezone-independent, which is why logs, databases and APIs store it.

Seconds, milliseconds or microseconds?

  • 10 digits — seconds (date +%s, PHP, Python time.time() truncated, JWT claims)
  • 13 digits — milliseconds (JavaScript Date.now(), Java)
  • 16 digits — microseconds (some databases and tracing systems)

The year 2038 problem

Systems that store Unix time in a signed 32-bit integer overflow at 2147483647 — 2038-01-19 03:14:07 UTC. Embedded devices and old firmware are the usual suspects.

Command line

Linux: date -d @1760277000 · macOS: date -r 1760277000 · PowerShell: [DateTimeOffset]::FromUnixTimeSeconds(1760277000)
